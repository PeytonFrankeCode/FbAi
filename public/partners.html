Questions

Who can apply?

Anyone who makes football card content or runs a card community: creators, breakers, Discord and forum owners, bloggers. You need a free Card Huddle account.

When do I get paid?

Each commission is held for 30 days in case of a refund, then it's ready. Once you have $25 ready, it's sent to your PayPal.

How long does a member count for me?

12 months from the day they first join from your link. Every payment they make in that year earns you 15%, monthly or yearly.

What doesn't count?

Refunded payments, and your own account. Signing yourself up from your own link doesn't earn anything.

Where do I see my numbers?

My Account, Partner program. It shows your link, clicks, sign-ups, what's held, what's ready and what's been paid.
