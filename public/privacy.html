Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 August 2026

The Card Huddle (thecardhuddle.com) is a free tool for looking up what football cards actually sell for. This page explains what we collect, why, who it goes to, and how to get rid of it. It describes what the site genuinely does — if something here does not match your experience, please tell us.

The short version

  • You can search card prices and browse every checklist without an account and without telling us anything about yourself.
  • If you make an account, we store a username, a password we cannot read, and optionally an email address.
  • Your collection, inventory and watchlist are yours. They stay in your browser unless you are signed in, in which case we also store a copy so your devices match.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and affiliate links to eBay and BCW. Each is described below.

What we collect

If you never make an account

You can use the price search, the checklists, the player and team guides and the market pages with no account. In that case what we hold is:

  • Analytics data — pages visited, roughly where in the world you are, and what kind of device and browser you use. Collected through Google Analytics, described below.
  • Things saved in your own browser — your theme choice, your recent searches, which banners you have dismissed, and any collection or watchlist you build. This lives in your browser's local storage. It is on your device, not our server, and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Ordinary server logs — our host, Cloudflare, records requests including IP address, for security and to keep the site running.

Payments

There are none. The Card Huddle is free, there is nothing to buy, and we do not collect payment details of any kind.

Advertising

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Your consent choices in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will see a consent prompt the first time you arrive. Until you answer it, advertising and analytics cookies are switched off — the site loads with ad storage, ad personalisation and analytics storage all denied, and nothing is written to your device for those purposes. If you decline, you still get the whole site; ads simply become non-personalised.

You can change your mind at any time:

The consent prompt is only shown to visitors in the regions listed above, so there is nothing to change from here.

Outside those regions, the opt-out links in the advertising section above are the way to turn off personalised ads.

Affiliate links

Links to eBay carry a tracking code, and our BCW links carry a discount code. If you buy after clicking one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This does not change which listings or prices we show you — sold prices come from real completed sales, and nothing about what we display is influenced by whether we would earn on it.

Who we share with

We do not sell your personal information. We share only with the service providers listed above, each doing a specific job for the site. We will also disclose information where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect the site or its users.

How long we keep things

  • Account data and your saved cards: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Sign-in sessions: they expire on their own, and immediately when you sign out.
  • Analytics: retained under Google Analytics' own retention settings.
  • Anything kept only in your browser: until you clear your browser data.

Your choices and rights

  • See or delete your data. Email us and we will send you what we hold on your account, or delete it.
  • Delete it yourself. Clearing your browser data removes everything stored on your device.
  • Change your consent choice if you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, using the button in the consent section above.
  • Opt out of ad personalisation using the links in the advertising section.
  • Opt out of analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights — for example under the UK and EU GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act — including the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to some processing. Ask us and we will honour those requests regardless of where you live.

Children

The Card Huddle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Traffic is served over HTTPS. No site can promise perfect security, and we would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced on the site.
