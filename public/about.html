About The Card Huddle

The Card Huddle is a free football card price tool. It answers one question as honestly as we can manage: what is this card actually selling for?

It is run by one person, not a company with a research department, and it is not affiliated with eBay, Panini, Topps, Fanatics, PSA, BGS or SGC. Nobody pays us to feature a card or a set.

What it does

  • Sold prices from real completed sales. Not asking prices, not estimates, not an algorithm's opinion of what something is worth. Prices here are what somebody actually paid.
  • Full checklists. Over 360 football products, more than 370,000 catalogued cards, with their parallels and inserts.
  • Grade breakdowns. A raw card and a PSA 10 of the same card are different markets, and they are shown separately where there is enough data to separate them.
  • Collection and inventory tracking, free, with or without an account.

Where the numbers come from

We maintain our own dataset of completed eBay sales for football cards, built continuously. That dataset is the source for every price on the site. It is not licensed from a price guide and it is not scraped from another tracker.

How a set or player page turns that into a figure — which average, over what period, and why some pages show no prices at all — is written out in full on the methodology page.

What it costs

Nothing. The site is supported by advertising and by affiliate commission when someone buys through a link to eBay, at no extra cost to the buyer. That is disclosed on every page carrying such a link, and it never changes which prices we show — the numbers come out of the sales data before any link is attached to them.

What it is not

It is not an appraisal service, and nothing here is financial advice. A median of past sales is a record of what happened, not a promise about what your card will fetch. Condition, timing, grading population and plain luck all move a real sale away from the number on the screen.

Getting in touch

Corrections are genuinely welcome — a wrong checklist entry, a price that looks off, a card attributed to the wrong player. See contact.
