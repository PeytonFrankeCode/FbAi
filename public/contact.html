Contact

One inbox, read by the person who runs the site:

cardhuddlecollectors@gmail.com

Especially useful

  • Data corrections. A card listed under the wrong player, a missing parallel, a checklist that does not match the set in your hands. These are the reports that improve the site most, and they are always welcome.
  • Prices that look wrong. If a median looks nothing like what you see on the market, tell us which card and we will look at the sales behind it. Sometimes the parser has mis-read a listing title; sometimes the market really has moved.
  • Missing sets. If a product you collect is not in the checklists, say so.
  • Account and data requests. Deleting your account or getting a copy of your data can be done from Settings, but email works too.
  • Rights and takedowns. If you believe something here infringes your rights, write in with the detail and we will deal with it.

What to expect

This is a one-person project, so replies are not instant — usually within a few days. Reports with a specific card, set or URL get answered faster, because they can be checked directly.

Advertising

For questions about ads on the site, or about the affiliate links disclosed in the footer, the same address reaches us.
