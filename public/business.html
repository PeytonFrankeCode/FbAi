Questions

Is The Card Huddle still free for collectors?

Yes, forever. Every collector feature stays free. Business is a separate tool for people who sell.

Where do the prices come from?

Completed eBay sales we track every day, matched to the exact card and grade. Not asking prices, and never a guess: a card needs 3 recent sales of the same grade to get a price.

Do I have to give you my eBay password?

No. Store sync reads your public listings by username. We never see your eBay account.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in one click from Billing. No contract.
